# Quantifying Quantum Advantage: An Empirical Benchmark of Boson Sampling

## 📝 Abstract
[[Quantum computers are very expensive and difficult to maintain. In such a scenario one has to ask themselves- are they even worth it? The answer depends on what you are trying to achieve. Quantum computers are not faster classical computers their job is to make those tasks possible that are impossible with the classical computer. They simulate nature. Nature is inherently quantum, and we need qubits to simulate it. There are certain tasks in which quantum computation outperforms classic computation. In this paper we have clearly compared the advantage of quantum computers over classical computers when it comes to analyzing and predicting the behavior of photons in a Boson sampling experiment. We offer a thorough comparison of the performance metrics of classical vs quantum simulation of Boson sampling. The classical simulator was approximately 100× slower than the quantum simulator at 6 photons. The gap continued to widen as we kept adding photons. Index Terms—Boson Sampling, Quantum Simulation, Quantum Advantage, Classical vs Quantum Computation]
